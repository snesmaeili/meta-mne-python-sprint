3. LAYER 3 -- explicit alignment; nothing happens implicitly
========================================================================
  pad()  -> uniform=True, nave(t) from 40 down to 1

  compute_tfr refuses an impossible frequency set:
    25 of 25 requested frequencies need a wavelet longer than the shortest epoch (477 > 271 samples).
    The shortest epoch bounds the whole set, because every epoch must yield the same frequency axis.
    n_cycles is proportional to freq here, so every wavelet has the same length and raising fmin will not help: lower n_cycles, or drop the shortest epochs.

  compute_tfr(average=False) -> <RaggedEpochsTFR | 40 epochs, 20 freqs (11-30 Hz), 271-570 samples, output=power>
  tfr.average() raises: Cannot average TFRs of unequal duration: time index k is not the same phase of the task in every trial. Align first:
